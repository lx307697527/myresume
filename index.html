Every project starts with a concrete business bottleneck. Below: the problem, the approach, the outcome — and what it was built with.

DTC · China → Europe · 5 weeks

Cross-border homeware brand: an AI agent takes over 68% of repeat questions

Next.js · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · pgvector · OpenAI · WhatsApp API · Cloudflare

Problem

A three-person support team took 12 hours on average to send a first reply in English and German. In peak season, 200+ messages a day arrived and roughly 70% were repeat questions. Sales were lost not to the product, but to slow replies.

What I built

Rebuilt the storefront on Next.js and turned the catalog, sizing tables, shipping and returns policies and past tickets into a searchable knowledge base. Connected WhatsApp, Instagram and email; low-confidence answers escalate to a human, and every reply carries its source and confidence. Staff now only handle disputes, custom orders and judgement calls.

Result

  • First response fell from 12 hours to under 2 minutes
  • 68% of repeat questions auto-resolved; humans handle the exceptions
  • About 1.5 FTE freed up; peak-season conversion up 19%
Manufacturing · China · 3 weeks

Ningbo hardware maker: turning tribal quoting knowledge into a system

Python · LangGraph · PostgreSQL · OCR · WeCom API

Problem

Every quote took 30 minutes and depended on one senior estimator's memory. New staff needed six months to quote alone; when material prices moved, old templates produced loss-making quotes. Inquiries arrived by email, WeChat and photos, with no consistent record.

What I built

Structured drawing parameters, material prices and process hours. OCR plus an LLM parses email and photo inquiries; a rules engine calculates cost, and anything below the margin threshold is forced to human review. Quotes are generated as PDFs inside WeCom, and every historical quote is stored and searchable by client and part.

Result

  • Quoting dropped from 30 minutes to under 3; 400+ inquiries a month
  • Margin alerts caught 11% of quotes that would have lost money
  • New staff quote independently after one week, no longer dependent on one estimator
SaaS MVP · Overseas · 6 weeks

B2B SaaS: from idea to a product that can charge, in 6 weeks

Next.js · tRPC · Prisma · PostgreSQL · Stripe · Cloudflare Workers

Problem

A non-technical founder needed to validate willingness to pay within three months, without hiring an engineering team first. The market window was short: the register → trial → pay loop had to work fast.

What I built

Scope was locked: multi-tenancy and roles, Stripe subscriptions and invoicing, email sequences, usage dashboards and a marketing site. A demo shipped every two weeks: monetisation first, polish second. Everything runs on Cloudflare with managed Postgres to keep operating costs minimal.

Result

  • Live in 6 weeks; 12 paying trials in the first month
  • ~$1,800 MRR in month one, validating willingness to pay
  • Closed an angel round in month four, then began hiring
Multi-site retail · China · 4 weeks

Restaurant chain: daily reporting without manual spreadsheets

WeChat mini-program · Node.js · Feishu Open Platform · Cloudflare D1

Problem

Eight stores reported numbers in a WeChat group. Head office spent three hours a day consolidating them, the figures rarely reconciled, and the owner always saw yesterday's data — anomalies surfaced too late.

What I built

Store staff fill in a mini-program form with validation on required fields and outlier values. At 9:00 every day a dashboard is generated automatically, with anomalies pushed to the store manager. A weekly AI summary lands in Feishu, and history is queryable by store and period.

Result

  • Consolidation dropped from 3 hours a day to 15 minutes
  • Inventory discrepancies down 40%; anomalies visible the same day
  • Head office stopped reconciling manually; managers get alerts directly

问题

我做了什么

重建 Next.js 独立站，把产品目录、尺码表、物流与退换政策、历史工单整理成可检索知识库。接入 WhatsApp、Instagram 和邮件三个通道；低置信度自动转人工，每条回答都带来源和置信度。客服只处理争议、定制和售后判断。

宁波五金制造：把老师傅的报价经验变成系统

Python · LangGraph · PostgreSQL · OCR · 企业微信 API

问题

每单报价要 30 分钟，全靠老师傅的经验。新人半年才能独立出单；材料价格一涨，旧模板就会报出亏本价。询价散落在邮件、微信和图片里，没人能统一复盘。

我做了什么

把图纸参数、材料价格和工艺工时结构化；用 OCR 加 LLM 解析邮件与图片询价，规则引擎算成本，毛利低于阈值时强制人工审批。报价单直接在企业微信里生成 PDF，历史报价进入数据库，按客户和产品可查。

结果

  • 报价从 30 分钟缩到 3 分钟内，每月处理 400+ 询价
  • 毛利预警拦下 11% 的低价单，避免直接亏损
  • 新人 1 周可以独立出单，不再依赖单一老师傅
